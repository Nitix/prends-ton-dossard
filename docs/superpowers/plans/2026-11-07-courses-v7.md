# « Prends ton dossard » v7 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A race says when its registration opens, to the hour, and each person who goes is reminded the day before and/or an hour before (calendar alarms and Web Push, as they choose in Réglages), once; races known only by their month; a title per page; zoom on the map and on the D+ profile; sharing a race (Web Share, or copy and WhatsApp); a group calendar feed; the weather of the race days; the mandatory gear and each person's private checklist; the MCP tools for all of it; then a dev environment on the server to try it for real.

**Architecture:** Seven migrations (`9l` to `9r`). New Go packages: `server/push` (VAPID keys in a file of the data directory, RFC 8291/8292 on the standard library, a sender limited to the four push services, the device subscriptions and their routes), `server/reminders` (each person's choice, its routes, and a PocketBase cron job every minute that sends the reminders that are due, at most once, recorded in `reminder_deliveries`), `server/weather` (Open-Meteo forecast and geocoding behind an in-memory cache, a per-hour budget and a route), `server/gear` (the checklist's hooks). The calendar package gains timed entries, alarms, month precision and a second feed scope (`group`). The service worker shows the notifications. The front gains the opening (form, hero, card, home), the reminders and push section of Réglages, month-only races everywhere, `usePageTitle`, the map and profile zoom, the share button and its fallback dialog, two calendar links, the weather section and the gear list. The MCP tools gain the new fields and three member tools (`reminders`, `get_weather`, `gear_checklist`).

**Tech Stack:** Go 1.27 + PocketBase v0.41.1, `github.com/modelcontextprotocol/go-sdk` v1.8.0 (already there), React 19, Vite 8, TanStack Router/Query, Tailwind 4 (« Crête » and the skins, light and dark), Leaflet 1.9.4, Vitest 5. **No new dependency**, Go or web.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-courses-v7-design.md`. Section numbers below (« spec § 4.3 ») are the spec's; « MCP spec § 6.13 » is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-courses-mcp-design.md`.

**Tasks** (the order of the ledger): **opening and reminders** 1 the opening on the server and in the MCP · 2 the opening in the site · 3 reminder choice and calendar alarms · 4 Web Push in Go · 5 device subscriptions and the service worker · 6 notifications in Réglages · 7 the scheduler — **month-only races** 8 server, logic and MCP · 9 the site — **10 page titles** — **zoom** 11 the map · 12 the profile — **13 sharing** — **14 the group calendar feed** — **weather** 15 server and MCP · 16 the site — **gear** 17 server, logic and MCP · 18 the site — **19 seed, docs, end to end, decisions** — **20 the dev environment** (controller only).

type Keys struct{ /* unexported */ }
